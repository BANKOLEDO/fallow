Fallow

Every file in your Downloads, sorted into keep, archive or delete.

Label about 200 files by hand. Fallow learns your calls, then sorts the rest on your own computer. It only suggests: it never moves, renames or deletes a file.

Field map of your files

One square per file. Point at a count to find its files.

How it works

Fallow reads each file's size, age, type, folder and name. It never opens the file itself. From the files you label, it learns what you tend to keep, archive or delete, and predicts the rest.

Classification runs entirely on your machine and costs nothing. Nothing leaves it unless you switch on an optional extra.
